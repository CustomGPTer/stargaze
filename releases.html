Release notes

What changed in each version of StarGaze. The newest is at the top.

Version 2.0.2 26 November 2026Latest

  • A real night sky after loading Gaia. Loading or opening a Gaia field no longer turns the whole sky into a bright glare. If your sky looked like that, this update puts it right.
  • Fainter stars appear as you zoom in. A normal view shows about what your eyes can see; pinch in to reveal fainter stars, which fade in dim and brighten as you keep zooming. The faintest Gaia stars appear at the deepest zoom.
  • The best-known stars, such as Sirius, Betelgeuse, Polaris, Vega, Capella and the Plough stars, are always named when they are in view. More names appear as you zoom in.
  • Bright stars no longer swell into large blobs when you zoom right in.
  • Loading the same Gaia field twice now updates it instead of saving a second copy, and existing copies are tidied away.

Version 2.0.1 26 November 2026

  • Gaia fixed. With optional online access on, “Load stars around my view” and the Gaia source search work again. Fields of up to 6,000 stars load, are saved for offline viewing and appear in the sky.
  • When the space for saved fields is full, the oldest saved field now makes room for the new one.

Version 2.0.0 26 November 2026

  • A new sky. Stars, constellation drawings, lines, the Moon, planets and the ground are now drawn together, sharper and smoother.
  • Constellation drawings are pinned to their stars and only the constellation in the middle of the screen is shown, so the sky is never cramped. Switch Centred only off in Layers to see them all.
  • Detail grows as you zoom: fainter stars, then galaxy, nebula and cluster markers, then their names. Labels no longer overlap.
  • 2.5 million stars offline, a real photograph of the Milky Way, and real photographs of 320 galaxies, nebulae and clusters that fade in as you zoom.
  • The Moon and planets as lit globes with detailed surfaces and true phases.
  • A real ground to stand on: a field with trees, rolling hills, open moor or a winter snowfield, or trace your own skyline with the camera.
  • A realistic sky: blue by day, sunset colours, stars appearing through twilight, and moonlight and light pollution brightening the sky.
  • A full-screen sky with small controls at the edges. Tap the sky to hide them. The globe button gives a round view of the whole sky.
  • A new time panel over the sky: step by an hour or a day, slide through the night, jump to sunset, darkness, moonrise, sunrise or the next full Moon, and speed time up.
  • Tap anything to see its brightness, height and rise and set times. An arrow at the edge of the screen points the way to the object you chose.
  • New: eyepiece view in the photo planner, red night mode that switches on after dark, smoother phone pointing and four new guided tours.
  • Installs over 1.0.1 and keeps your journal, list and settings.

Version 1.0.1 26 November 2026

  • First public release. 119,625 stars, 89,868 deep-sky objects with all 110 Messier objects, and the 88 constellations with lines, boundaries, asterisms and western artwork, all offline.
  • The Sun, Moon and planets for any date from 1900 to 2100, now drawn at their true size when you zoom in, with the Moon's lit side facing the Sun.
  • Tonight now shows the Moon at tonight's phase, with sunset, full darkness and the best targets above your horizon.
  • Phone pointing, and an optional camera view that now fills the screen without stretching, works sideways too, and matches the camera's field of view.
  • Search, 24-hour altitude views, a sky calendar with reminders, four guided journeys, a photo planner and a private observing journal with photos.
  • Optional online extras, all off until you switch them on: Gaia DR3 stars, SIMBAD, NASA JPL objects, survey photos, weather, satellite passes and 37 more sky cultures.
  • New: Check for updates, More apps and the StarGaze website in Settings and About, and "Developed by Jonathan Jackson ©".
  • New: an adaptive app icon with a themed version, and a proper reminder icon (reminders showed a blank square).
  • Fixes: the selected tab now shows a pill behind its icon on a solid bottom bar; a cancelled touch no longer stops the next drag; opening a saved Gaia field zooms to that field; Earth no longer shows a made-up sky position; a late location time-out no longer cancels a newer request; satellite passes still calculate if the phone's WebView cannot start a background task; changing dark mode, display size or split screen no longer restarts the app.

Checking your download

The file for version 2.0.2 is called StarGaze.apk. It is 60.6 MB (63,583,943 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

94561c6599819ed4f3fc0ecfaf3bd799f8117077fbf3d1e8af54fa9d50a49070

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile StarGaze.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.
