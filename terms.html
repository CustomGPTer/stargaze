Positions and predictions

StarGaze works out positions carefully, but phone sensors, clocks and catalogue data all have limits. Satellite passes ignore whether a satellite is lit. Weather is a general forecast, not a measure of seeing. Use StarGaze as a guide, not for navigation or anything where safety is at stake.

Never look at the Sun

Never look at the Sun, with or without binoculars or a telescope, without certified solar protection. StarGaze shows the Sun's position only so you can avoid it.

Online services

Gaia data is from ESA's Gaia mission, processed by DPAC. SIMBAD and survey images come from CDS and STScI, ephemerides from NASA JPL Horizons, weather from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0) and orbital data from CelesTrak.
