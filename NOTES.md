StarGaze 2.0.1

A fix for the optional Gaia DR3 online catalogue.

- "Load stars around my view" and the Gaia source-identifier search work again: fields of up to 6,000 stars load, are saved for offline viewing and appear in the sky.
- When the space for saved fields is full, the oldest saved field now makes room for the new one.

Installs over 2.0.0 and 1.0.1 and keeps your journal, list and settings.

StarGaze.apk is 60.6 MB (63,583,943 bytes). Android 11 or later.

Website: https://customgpter.github.io/stargaze/
