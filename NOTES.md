StarGaze 2.0.2

A real night sky again, with stars that appear as you zoom.

- Loading or opening a Gaia field no longer turns the whole sky into a bright glare. If your sky looked like that, this update puts it right.
- Fainter stars appear as you zoom in. A normal view shows about what your eyes can see; pinch in to reveal fainter stars, which fade in dim and brighten as you keep zooming. The faintest Gaia stars appear at the deepest zoom.
- The best-known stars, such as Sirius, Betelgeuse, Polaris, Vega, Capella and the Plough stars, are always named when they are in view. More names appear as you zoom in.
- Bright stars no longer swell into large blobs when you zoom right in.
- Loading the same Gaia field twice now updates it instead of saving a second copy, and existing copies are tidied away.

Installs over 2.0.1, 2.0.0 and 1.0.1 and keeps your journal, list and settings.

StarGaze.apk is 60.6 MB (63,583,943 bytes). Android 11 or later.
