What StarGaze does not do

StarGaze is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site. It has no telescope control, its offline catalogue is 119,625 stars rather than millions, and it does not have a full lunar atlas or every comet and asteroid offline.

Where the others do more

Stellarium Plus carries millions of stars and deep-sky objects offline, high-resolution images and telescope control. Star Walk 2 points out satellites, the ISS, the Hubble Space Telescope, asteroids and comets as you move your phone. Both are on Google Play.

Photos and globes

Planet globes and the Milky Way in StarGaze are illustrations. Survey photos are real telescope images, loaded only when you ask.
