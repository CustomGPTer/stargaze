Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the StarGaze app for Android (package com.jonjackson.stargaze) and this website. StarGaze is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • There is no account, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives nothing from the app.
  • The sky and everything you keep in the app work offline. Online access is off until you switch it on.
  • When it is on, each service you use sees your IP address and what that request needs. Your journal is never sent.
  • Your journal, list and settings stay on your phone, and phone backups leave them out.

Each service handles what it receives under its own policy. There is no StarGaze server, so nothing is sent to the developer. Switching online access off stops new requests and disconnects any in progress.

Location

Choosing a town or typing coordinates needs no permission. Use device location asks Android for one position while the app is open. StarGaze saves it as your observing place and stops listening. It never asks for background location.

Camera and sensors

The camera view is optional. It shows your camera's picture behind the sky and records nothing: no photos, no video and no sound. Phone pointing uses the phone's orientation sensors, which need no permission.

What stays on your phone

Your journal (with any photos you attach, made smaller and without their hidden information), observing list, settings, saved Gaia fields and downloaded data are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's cloud backup is switched off for StarGaze. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

What the app can reach

Internet (only for the online features you switch on), location (only when you ask), camera (only for the camera view), notifications (only for reminders you set) and starting after a restart (to keep your reminders). No microphone, contacts or background location.

Backups and sharing

Export backup saves a file with your journal, photos, list and settings where you choose. It is not encrypted: keep it private. Nothing is exported unless you ask.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

StarGaze collects nothing from anyone, and has nothing in it unsuitable for children.
